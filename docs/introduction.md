# Introduction

The Spatac workbook is the spreadsheet the Antiquary board (MTCC No. 1360) and SPATAC, the
Special Projects and Technical Advisory Committee, use to work with the reserve fund. It brings
together:

- the Reserve Fund Study (RFS), as the vendor's own spreadsheet;
- Spatac's decisions about when projects happen and what they cost;
- the reserve fund's investments, held and planned;
- the Investment Plan the Reserve Fund Investment Policy requires (s.3.2).

The workbook has five jobs, each with a chapter in this guide:

1. [Analysing the Reserve Fund Study](purposes/analyse-rfs.md). The vendor supplies the RFS as
   a spreadsheet without formulas, so its figures can be examined item by item and year by
   year rather than read from a PDF.
2. [Validating the RFS funding proposal](purposes/validate-funding.md). The workbook checks
   that the study's interest, inflation and planning-horizon assumptions do not lead to larger
   contribution increases than necessary.
3. [Tracking project timing and cost](purposes/track-projects.md). It records what Spatac and
   the Board decide about each project.
4. [The Investment Plan](purposes/investment-plan.md). It matches investment maturities and cash
   to the expenditures expected over the next five years.
5. [Estimating future contribution increases](purposes/contribution-increases.md). It estimates
   what increases the next RFS will need to keep the fund above its minimum.

The [procedures](procedures/rollover.md) cover what to do each January, when a new RFS arrives,
and when the workbook is edited in more than one application. Two command-line tools, described
under [Command-line tools](tools/install.md), compare two versions of the workbook and extract
the Investment Plan as a workbook for the Board and the broker.

Who reads what:

- **Board and SPATAC members** need [The workbook at a glance](workbook.md) and the five
  purpose chapters.
- **Whoever maintains the workbook** also needs the procedures and the tools.
