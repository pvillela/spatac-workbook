**Settings:** B8 `Cfg_ShortInflationYears` = 3 (unchanged); new B9 `Cfg_ShortInterestYears` = 3,
"Years of short-term interest in the RFS (the long-term rate applies after them)"; note in A10.
Interest is short-term for `RFS_StudyYear` … `RFS_StudyYear` + B9 − 1 (2024–2026).

**Projected_Balances** (rebuilt):
- Inputs O4:P10, labels right-justified in O: P4 start year (`=Cfg_BaseYear`, calculated), P5
  opening balance (`PB_OpeningBalance`), P6 "Contribution increases follow the RFS schedule
  through" (`PB_ContribFixedThrough`, 2027), P7:P9 catch-up increases (`PB_CatchUp`), P10
  subsequent increase (`PB_SubsequentIncrease`, default `=RFS_SubsequentIncrease`).
- Summaries over the first 30 years: B4:G7 (RFS, `PB_RFSLowest`) and R4:W7 (what-if, `PB_Lowest`):
  label, then Lowest, Year, First negative, Year (`MINIFS`; "none" when never negative).
- Row 12 block headings; H12:I12 "Years that differ" count (red when not 0). Row 13 headings.
  Years 2024–2073 in rows 14–63; 2054–2073 (rows 44–63) light grey. Panes frozen below row 13.
- A year. RFS block B–M (`PB_RFS*`): opening, costs (read), increase (read: K9:K11 then K12),
  contributions (C12 grown), rate (read, G7 then G8), interest, closing, **I: difference from
  RFS_cashflow G** (orange above 0.5), three margins, per unit per month. N spacer. What-if O–Z
  (`PB_Opening` … `PB_PerUnitMonth`): same columns; V is the difference from the RFS block's
  balance. AA spacer; AB inflator (folded).
- The what-if always starts in the current year, Revised expenditures; its increase is the RFS's
  through P6, then P7:P9, then P10; its first contribution grows from the RFS block's previous
  year. The three "RFS" choices and `PB_Start`, `PB_ExpBasis`, `PB_ContribBasis`,
  `PB_StartBalance` are gone. Every what-if number and lowest margin equals
  `Spatac-20261124f.xlsx`'s.
- Today: the recomputed RFS matches RFS_cashflow in all 50 years (I12 = 0); what-if lowest margin
  over minimum −1,882,352 (2050), first negative −1,058,672 (2045); RFS lowest −73,672 (2050).
