## Hard rules learned (all sessions)

Everything in `HANDOFF-20261122.md`'s list still holds (`_nogit` create-only; `Name-YYYYMMDD` +
letter names by Eastern date; name the workbook before extracting the plan; literal paths;
readable formulas; protection per cell; commit trailer). New this session:

- **Commit trailer** is now
  `Claude-Session: https://claude.ai/code/session_01Xeq4mEKT1aYQVZhFPsgxEM`.
- **While questions are open, send only the questions.** No summaries or extra reading until
  they are answered (memory `questions-first`).
- **Qualify every cell reference with its tab and workbook**, and don't re-ask what the user's
  prompt document already says (memory `qualify-cell-refs`).
- **Ask from the expected outcome**, not from what a tool did or did not do.
- **"Typed"** is ambiguous: say "already in the sheet" or "entered by the user".
- **The golden source is the user's**: `_nogit/Spatac-L-20261123.xlsx`. Numeric parity with it is
  required. Reasons for change are the user's, on the Adjustments tab; Claude's inferred reasons
  (old column U) are gone.
- **Write outputs to `_nogit` for review**; the user cannot review a scratchpad build.
- **Prefer Calc's own operations** (moving ranges, inserting columns) over rewriting formulas by
  hand, when they do the job.
- **Groups that touch merge in `.xlsx`**; a cell in a foldable column may end up hidden.
- **Never leave a lock file in `_nogit`**: scripts open sources with `Session.open_copy`.
- **Comments hold provenance, not instructions** (design 14.9), with one user-requested exception
  (Expenditures T6).

## Workbook facts (as built; design 14 and 14.12)

**Expenditures:** A–I unchanged; J adjustment label (folded alone); K spacer; L–P current year
(Approved, Actuals to date, Mid-year, Late-year, Current estimate; not grouped); Q working notes
(they name columns by heading, in quotes); R spacer; S next year (S2 the year, bold); T spacer
holding the block's description (T2) and the do-not-modify comment (T6); U–AX adjusted RFS
2024–2053 (`Exp_Adjusted`), past years folded. Years: numbers in U6:AX6 (`Exp_AdjustedYears`,
green, invisible), text headings in row 7. Rows: 3 RFS total, 4 totals (Q4 "Adjusted total"),
5 variance = RFS − Spatac (negative red), 6 provenance/years; items 8–249; ▼ End 250. Blank vs 0:
blank when the RFS has no amount and no adjustment touches the year. Orange shading: differs from
the RFS by more than 0.5.

**Adjustments:** four blocks (titles `RFS_StudyYear`+0…3) at rows 4, 20, 36, 52; 10 input rows
each; A Item (dropdown from `Exp_AdjLabel`), B/C Year from/to ("none" or 2024–2053, from hidden
J `Adj_YearChoices`), D Amount (>0; from-year dollars, to-year when from is "none"), E Reason,
F/G from/to amounts, H Check (OK, right-justified). A2 counts problems. The user's six rows are
in rows 39–44. Factor: `PB_Inflator` (two rates, `Cfg_ShortInflationYears` = 3 on Settings B8).

**Medium_Term_Cashflow:** blank row 24; 25 "Minimum closing cash balance"; 29–31 RFS vs Revised
(29 reads `RFS_Fv_Totals`); 33–35 balance comparison. B9: base year from `Exp_Current`, later
years from `Exp_Adjusted`.

**Projected_Balances K:** base year `Exp_Current`; other years to 2053 `Exp_Adjusted`; before the
base year and after 2053, the RFS (B).

**The one departure from parity:** 3.9 Garage Ramp, 2028 = 243,547 (229,500 × 1.02³), not the
golden 238,772; a comment on its 2028 cell says so.
